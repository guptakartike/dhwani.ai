📊 Multi-Strategy Vector Indexing Strategies

To ensure comprehensive context retrieval across complex Hindi queries, dhwani.ai indexes documents using three complementary chunking strategies:

1. Passage Chunking

Indexes complete paragraphs to preserve macro context and overarching topic semantics for broad questions.

2. Sentence Window

Captures individual sentences expanded with a ±1 sliding sentence window for fine-grained factual accuracy.

3. Semantic Sentence

Groups sentences dynamically based on vector cosine similarity thresholds to isolate coherent semantic units.
